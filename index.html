About this Directory & Methodology

Definitions & Scope

  • News Sources: Established media outlets, state broadcasters, and recognized digital-native publishers with formal editorial processes.
  • Chatter Sources: Gossip blogs, influential social media aggregators, and alternative media driving local citizen discourse.

Hybrid Positioning & Link Policy

Certain channels (e.g., Sahara Reporters) are designated as Hybrid. These bridge direct investigative reporting with crowd-sourced social chatter, making them essential for real-time risk evaluation.

Linking Strategy: News profiles target official websites; Chatter profiles link directly to their most active social feed or official website, depending on which represents the higher-velocity observation surface for real-time monitoring.
